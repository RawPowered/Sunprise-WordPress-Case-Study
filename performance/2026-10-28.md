These are lab results for a temporary, incomplete staging restoration, not historical measurements of sunprise.com. The original slider's video files were absent; slider posters and some homepage images were restored. Missing media and differences in hosting, configuration, and content can affect results. No real-user field data was available. The mobile audit identified render-blocking resources and a large page payload among opportunities. Do not describe these numbers as the original site's performance or as results for a fully restored site.
